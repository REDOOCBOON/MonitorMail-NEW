MonitorMail

Privacy Policy

MonitorMail is a tool for faculty advisors at SRM Institute of Science and Technology to notify students (and their parents) about low attendance.

Who can use MonitorMail

Only faculty with an official @srmist.edu.in email who have verified their email and been approved by the administrator.

What we store

  • Teacher accounts: name, email, a salted hash of the password (never the password itself), approval status.
  • Student directory: registration number, name, section, department, student and parent email/phone, as provided by the department.
  • Attendance reports: uploaded PDFs are read in memory to find students below 75% and are not stored.
  • Email history: which student was emailed, when, by which teacher, and the message text.
  • Security log: sign-in attempts and administrative actions with IP address.

Google account data (Gmail)

When a teacher clicks Connect Gmail, MonitorMail asks Google for permission to:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): used only to send the attendance notifications and announcements that you review and send yourself, from your own address.
  • See your email address (openid, email): used only to confirm the connected Google account matches your MonitorMail account.

MonitorMail cannot read, search, or delete any email in your mailbox. The permission is stored encrypted and is used only when you send.

MonitorMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not read by humans.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared. It is stored with our hosting providers (Render for the application, Neon for the database) only to run the service.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect Gmail at any time in MonitorMail (Account → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your teacher account or a student record, contact the administrator below.
